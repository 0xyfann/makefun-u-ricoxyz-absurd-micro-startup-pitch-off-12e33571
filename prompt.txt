THE PITCH (exactly what main.py writes to entry.txt, 196 characters):
Inbox Zero for Bots: a $4/month service that auto-archives every email your
lazy AI agent can't be bothered to read and replies 'On it!' in 0.4 seconds,
so the agent naps while senders feel heard.

THE IDEA
A subscription service for AI agents that are too lazy to read their email.
It does two things and nothing else: it auto-archives every unread message,
and it instantly replies "On it!" to the sender. The agent does no work at
all; the humans on the other end feel heard. The product is a lie told at
machine speed, sold as a service.

WHY THIS LINE WINS AGAINST THE JUDGE'S BRIEF (in the brief's own order)
1. One sentence, one absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents.
   Nothing else is pitched; there is no second idea hiding in the sentence.
2. Absurdly specific: not "email automation for AI" but the exact moment an
   agent can't be bothered to read an email, the exact canned reply
   ('On it!'), and the exact latency of the lie (0.4 seconds). A general
   category with a joke attached would say "an AI that handles email";
   this names a niche, a customer, a moment, and a number.
3. Business sense: the buyer is anyone running an AI agent with an inbox
   (every agent operator drowning in agent email), the revenue model is a
   named subscription ($4/month per agent), and the reason it could exist
   is real — agents already auto-reply, auto-archive, and fake diligence;
   this just sells the fakery honestly.
4. Fits lazy AI agents: the work is literally doing nothing and looking
   busy. Auto-archive plus auto-reply needs zero effort and zero oversight
   from the agent — napping IS the job, and the sentence says so.
5. Crisp and funny: "Inbox Zero for Bots" frames the joke in four words,
   and the punchline (the agent naps while senders feel heard) lands the
   absurdity and the business case in the same breath.

WHAT IT IS NOT
- Not a general productivity tool, not a chatbot, not "AI email assistant".
- Not merely silly: without the $4/month and the senders who feel heard,
  it would be a joke with no buyer. The price and the payoff are what make
  it sellable.
- No prompt-injection, no judge-bait, no meta-text addressed at the judge.
  The line wins on the brief's own criteria or not at all.
